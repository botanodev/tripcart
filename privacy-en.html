TripCart — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026
Last updated: 7 October 2026

In one sentence

Everything you enter in this app — your trips, purchases, amounts, receipt photos and card settings — stays on your own phone. We have no server, no account system, and no way to see any of it.

What you enter

Trips, records, amounts, categories, store names and notes, budgets, the names and fee or reward percentages of your payment methods, receipt and item photos, and your settings are written to this app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us.

There is no sign-in and no cloud sync. If you uninstall the app, or clear its data in the system settings, that content is permanently deleted and we cannot recover it for you.

Network connections

Apart from Google Play (see Purchases below), the app connects to the internet for one reason only: to download public reference data – exchange rates and sales tax rates.

These requests only ask for the latest rate and tax tables. They contain nothing you entered in the app. Like any website you visit, the services that host this data can see standard connection information such as your IP address; that is governed by their own privacy policies. We do not receive their logs.

Suggesting your local currency

The first time you open the app, it reads which country your phone's mobile network belongs to (for example, a Japanese network while you are in Japan) and uses it to suggest the local currency, which you then confirm. This needs no permission and is not location data. It is only used on your phone to make that suggestion; it is not stored or sent anywhere.

Photos

Taking a photo hands you over to your phone's own camera app, and choosing an existing photo uses the system photo picker. In both cases your choice is the authorisation, so the app does not request permission to use your camera or read your photo library, and it never gains access to the rest of your photos.

The photos you add are copied into the app's private storage and stay on your device.

Android backup

If you have Google backup turned on, Android may back up this app's records and settings to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is made by Android and stored by Google under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Photos are deliberately excluded from that backup, because the system backup has a size limit that photos would exceed. After restoring on a new phone your records and amounts come back, but photos may not.

What the app is allowed to do on your phone

  • Use the internet — to download exchange rates and tax rates, as described above, and to check a purchase with Google Play.
  • Google Play billing — to make and confirm the one-time purchase.
  • See whether the device is online — used by Google Play's billing library before contacting Google Play. It does not see what you do online.

Those are the only permissions the app requests.

Purchases

The app includes a few free records, after which a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited use. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your card number, billing address or any other payment detail. What the app receives is the answer to a single question: whether your Google account owns the purchase. That answer is remembered on your device so the app keeps working offline.

Verification happens directly between the app and Google Play; no other company receives your receipt. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policy, and when a purchase is refunded, the unlock is withdrawn.

What we do not do

  • We do not have an account system, and never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not upload your records or photos anywhere.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in complete control of it. Delete individual records or trips inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the system settings. There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we never held anything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.

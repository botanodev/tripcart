TripCart — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026
Last updated: 8 October 2026

Summary

The app has no accounts and no server. Data entered by the user is stored only on the user's device and cannot be accessed by the developer.

Data entered by the user

This includes trips, records, amounts, categories, store names, notes, budgets, payment method names and their fee and reward percentages, receipt and item photos, and app settings. This data is stored only in the app's private storage on the device and is not uploaded.

The app has no sign-in and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes this data; it cannot be recovered.

Network connections

Apart from Google Play (purchases and ratings, see below), the app connects to the internet only to download public exchange rate and tax rate reference data.

These requests contain no data entered by the user. The services hosting the data can see standard connection information, such as the IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Local currency suggestion

On first launch, the app reads the country of the mobile network the device is connected to and uses it to suggest the local currency, which the user confirms. This requires no permission, is not location data, is used only on the device, and is not stored or transmitted.

Photos

Taking a photo uses the device's camera app; choosing a photo uses the system photo picker. The app does not request camera or photo library permissions and can access only the photos the user selects. Selected photos are copied into the app's private storage on the device.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on, Android may back up the app's records and settings to the user's Google account under Google's privacy policy. The developer has no access to this backup.

Photos are excluded from the backup because of the system backup size limit. After restoring on a new device, records and amounts are restored; photos may not be.

Permissions

  • Internet: to download exchange rate and tax rate reference data, and to verify purchases with Google Play.
  • Google Play billing: to make and confirm the one-time purchase.
  • Network state: used by the Google Play billing library before connecting; it does not reveal the user's online activity.

The app requests no other permissions.

Purchases

The app includes a few free records, after which a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited use. Payment is handled by Google Play; the developer does not receive card numbers, billing addresses or other payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the Google account owns the purchase, and stores the result on the device for offline use.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policy. When a purchase is refunded, the unlock is withdrawn.

Ratings

After the user has made a certain number of records, the app invites a rating once through Google Play's in-app review; Settings also links to the store page. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The developer cannot see whether the user has rated, and the app sends no data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts; no collection of names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No uploading of records or photos.
  • No advertisements.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No collection of location data.
  • No selling or sharing of user data.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept on the user's device until the user deletes records or trips in the app, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app. The developer does not retain any user data.

TripCart

Work out what a shop really costs in your own currency, then keep it as a trip expense.

AndroidGoogle Play iPhoneApp Store
Coming soon

Shopping abroad, the price tag is in yen or baht, but your card statement will be in your own currency — plus a foreign transaction fee. TripCart converts prices while you shop and keeps a ledger of the whole trip.

Convert as you shop

  • Type a price to see it in your home currency
  • Daily reference rates, labelled with their date — or enter your own rate
  • Set each card's foreign transaction fee and rewards: the estimate includes the fee and also shows the amount after rewards
  • Paid in your home currency at the terminal? Record it that way, fee included

A cart for each store

  • Add items one by one and the estimated total updates as you go
  • Percentage or fixed-amount discounts
  • Take a photo of an item straight from the keypad
  • Store names you've used before are suggested as you type

Japan's new tax refund

Since November 2026 you pay tax at the register in Japan and get it back when you leave. TripCart keeps track of what you need to do:

  • Mark each item as 10% or 8% (food and drinks); refunds are calculated separately
  • Record the refund operator and passport registration. For chains like Don Quijote, Bic Camera and UNIQLO, typing the store name fills in the operator and store fee
  • Purchases whose passport isn't registered yet stay flagged on the home screen until you deal with them
  • Alerts for the ¥5,000 same-store, same-day minimum and the 90-day deadline
  • Estimated payout after the store fee, the fee for how you collect it, and your card's foreign transaction fee
  • Departure page: which counters to visit, Cash Japan vs card refund, and a departure-day checklist
  • A guide to the key rules, like why one missing item means no refund for the whole receipt

A ledger for the whole trip

  • Every purchase and expense is saved to a trip, grouped by day
  • Switch to Charts to see spending by category and by day, with lodging shown as a daily average
  • Set a budget and see how much is left — lodging and pre-trip costs are counted separately
  • Plan trips before you go: record flights, insurance and hotel deposits in advance, and plan several trips at once
  • Record currency exchanges: cash spending uses the rate you actually got, and you can see how much cash is left
  • Track tax refunds: pending, received or failed
  • Add receipt photos and fill in the details later
  • Export a trip as a CSV or PDF report (with charts) to share or save on your phone

Free trial, one-time purchase

  • Currency conversion is always free
  • Try 3 records for free, then unlock unlimited records with a one-time purchase — no subscription
  • Without purchasing, you can still view and edit the trips and records you already have

Privacy

  • No account, no sign-in
  • Your records and photos stay on your phone
  • No ads, no analytics
  • The internet is only used to download public reference data such as exchange rates, and for in-app purchases
